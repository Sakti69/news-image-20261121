`news-image-20261121`: one image that sums up the world's news of
Saturday 21 November 2026.

## The image

A one-page newspaper, drawn entirely in code by `main.py` (Python 3.13,
standard library only, no network):

- **Lead:** Russian missiles land in Poland as Ukraine endures its
  biggest barrage — 400+ drones and 110+ missiles overnight, three
  Russian weapons down in Poland, a NATO member.
- **Weather:** Cyclone Akvan slams India's east coast — 300,000+ moved
  to safety in Odisha.
- **Markets:** Wall Street slumps; the S&P 500 enters correction.
- **Climate:** the COP31 draft drops the words "fossil fuels".
- **Diplomacy:** Trump, Xi and Putin leave the Shenzhen summit with
  little to show — a day later, Moscow's barrage followed.

The four-frame hero band is drawn procedurally: missile trails over a
night map of Europe, the cyclone spiral, the plunging market chart, and
the summit hall of flags.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Two runs write byte-identical `entry.png` files (1024x1024 PNG).
